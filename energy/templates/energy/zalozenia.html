METHOD

How we estimate a full year from 35 days of history

Same model, longer weather record: the same event simulator generates both the history and annual consumption, using 12 months of observed historical weather for the latter. Their overlapping hours match by construction.
Heating seasonality: follows hourly temperature; the heat pump runs much longer in winter and not at all in summer. Lighting seasonality: follows monthly dusk times in Copenhagen. PV seasonality: follows observed hourly solar irradiance.
Limits of the method: we do not know actual holidays, illness or meter readings. The annual total is a scenario estimate, not a billing forecast. PV output excludes shading and the roof's exact azimuth; these are approximated by the 0.8 performance ratio.
Battery balance and daily use: each hour, PV output first serves household demand, then the battery supplies any shortfall, followed by the grid. Surplus PV charges the battery subject to its capacity and power limits; the rest is exported. Average daily use and weekly daily totals come from the same annual hourly data.
Year boundary: for the full-year calculation, the battery's initial charge is found by repeating the same model year until its charge at the year boundary stabilizes. This avoids an artificial first-night grid purchase if the battery would have charged in the preceding year. Shorter periods start with an empty battery.
System size: users can explore 1–150 kWp manually. The 100% coverage search uses that range; the shortest-payback search compares 1–15 kWp in 0.1 kWp steps. Large PV systems and seasonal batteries are theoretical scenarios. The calculator does not check roof area or installation feasibility and applies a linear €1,300/kWp cost at larger sizes too.
Battery and payback limits: the entered battery price is included in the investment; the 1% annual operating cost applies only to PV. Battery degradation, replacement, maintenance and grid charging are not modelled. A battery does not generate energy, and 100% coverage may be impossible with insufficient PV output, battery capacity or battery power.
